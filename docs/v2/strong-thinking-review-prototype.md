# Cross-family review and one rewrite (ST3 prototype)

ST3 is explicit on a versioned synthetic plan. Existing ST2 plans still stop at
`st3_required`; ordinary cards and default schema activation are unchanged.
Only the existing local Fake adapter is eligible. No provider, paid or T0
execution, real model quality claim, release readiness, or ST4 report is
enabled here.

The strong template requires one review of the accepted synthesis. The light
template uses its accepted answer. Stage ordering requires the exact sealed
source and original fixed evidence. A stored source pin binds the run/revision
and plan hash, accepted artifact, actual settled attempt route, canonical
concrete model/account identity, author seat and evidence digest. A registry
family relabel cannot turn that same identity into an independent reviewer. A planned route alone or a model's prose cannot supply an
author family. Review inputs are anonymous and come from the exact bound
source, facts and independently materialized approved Git bytes. If a blinded
payload contains a known exact author/reviewer channel or model token, it is
held without rewriting sealed bytes. This is not full free-text anonymity.

The author and reviewer candidate need explicit matching registered synthetic
channel and positioning family. Different accounts, channel names, or model
names do not prove independence; known different families and distinct concrete
routes/models do. Distinct eligible channels can share a synthetic account, in
which case they share quota; this is not independent-account review. ST3
rejects unknown or same family (including case variants, another channel, or
another model), an unavailable model, or an
unproved/exhausted synthetic quota window. It rechecks at reservation, claim and
the final start edge. There is no generic route fallback. An exact synthetic
account/window quote holds tokens transactionally across opted-in stages,
parallel intents, and runs sharing the account identity. The lower of explicit
remaining-ratio capacity and limit minus used tokens caps new holds before local
holds and settled spend are applied. Known authenticated
spend uses the historical window even on route mismatch, without accepting
the result; unknown/orphaned usage remains held. Known elapsed windows do not
charge known historic spend to a verified fresh window, but changing a label
or reset while an earlier window is active cannot free budget. A final
quota/route denial releases only the exact lease proven to have never started.
These are local fixture quotas, not provider quota or billing claims. Review acceptance
rechecks the actual consumed attempt, and a changed source cannot unlock the
rewrite. The rewrite is an explicitly approved slot using only the exact
actual author route, seat and historically measured synthetic account, not a list
of alternatives at runtime. Each dispatchable author route must already have
its exact rewrite counterpart approved before dispatch. Historical malformed
plans fail closed without reconstructing their approval. The same account can
rewrite in a proved new window;
reassigning the channel to another account does not inherit author authority.
Ordinary ST2 cancellation does not require the additional ST3 tables.

The reviewer provides a structured recomputation, denominator, threshold
direction, facts, uncertainty and counterexample, then zero to eight numbered
major/minor issues. The sole rewrite must disposition every numbered issue as
fixed or dissent with rationale. Major dissent remains unresolved; incomplete
or malformed output is blocked rather than retried. Successful terminal states
are explicitly synthetic and cannot be overwritten by later cancellation. The validation checks structure and custody, not the
truth of a real provider's claims.

Focused synthetic fixtures use two committed evidence files across independent
runs: denominator 25 rather than 20 changes the 75% decision, and a computed
25% increase violates the 20% cap. They test same-family aliases, missing
family, expired quota between claim and start, disappeared reviewer pins,
actual route mismatch, missing issue responses, retained major dissent and
duplicate rewrite requests. Prototype installation still requires typed
T1 v15 and does not change the global schema stamp.
